01 / THE PROBLEM

Your brand can be good and still be hard to understand.

Growing businesses rarely run out of things to say. They run into a different problem: everything starts saying something slightly different.

The website says one thing. The founder says another. The sales deck is three months behind. Content is everywhere, but the central idea is nowhere.

More content won't fix an unclear brand.

A QUESTION I KEEP ASKING

When someone hears your name, what should they remember?
